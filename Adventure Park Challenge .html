Meanings & Origins Behind the Gear

Carabiner: Comes from the German word Karabinerhaken, meaning "carbine hook." They were invented in the 19th century for German cavalry soldiers to quickly latch rifles to their shoulder straps on horseback.

Zipline: Long before becoming thrill rides, "tyrolean traverses" were invented in remote mountain ranges like the Alps and Andes as practical freight lines to shuttle food and workers across deep ravines.

Adrenaline: Comes from the Latin ad ("near") and renes ("kidneys"). The thrill hormone gets its name simply because the adrenal glands sit directly on top of your kidneys.

Park Fun Facts & Trivia

S.C.U.B.A.stands for Self-Contained Underwater Breathing Apparatus. It originated as a United States Navy military acronym in 1952 before transitioning into an everyday lowercase word.
