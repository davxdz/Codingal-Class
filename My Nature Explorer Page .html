Know Whats Best for Mother Nature

Plant native species

Grow local flora in gardens or pots to support regional pollinators like bees and birds.

Conserve water

Take shorter showers, fix leaking faucets promptly, and turn off the tap while brushing teeth.

Lower energy use

Unplug electronics when not in use ("phantom loads") and switch to energy-efficient LED bulbs.

Stop overconsumption

Think twice before buying single-use products or fast fashion; aim to reduce before you reuse or recycle.

Eliminate single-use plastics

Opt for reusable bottles, bags, and containers to keep oceans and landscapes free of synthetic debris.
