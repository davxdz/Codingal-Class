How to Improve Our Diet

To build a healthier diet, prioritize nutrient-rich whole foods like fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and whole grains while minimizing processed items and added sugars. Additionally, keep your portions balanced and stay well-hydrated throughout the day to support steady energy and overall digestion.
