Planet Earth - Quick Guide

Our planet Earth is full of incredible records, speciea, and facts. Here is a quick guide using lists!

Steps to Portect Our Planet:

  1. Switch of lights when you leave a room
  2. Use reusable water bottle everyday
  3. Walk or cycle instead of travelling by car
  4. Plant a tree in yoyur neighborhood
  5. Spread awareness - talk to your friends!

Incredible Natural Wonders:

  • Amazon Rainforest - home to 10 percent of all species on earth
  • Great Bareer Reef - the largest coral reef, visible from space
  • Mount Everest - the highest point on earth at 8,849 meters
  • Sahara Desert - the largest hot desert in the world

Key Terms:

Climate Change
Long term shifts in global temperature caused by human activity
Renewable Energy
energy from natural sources like sunlight and wind that never run out
Biodiversity
The variety of all living species on Earth
