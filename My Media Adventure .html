My Media Adventure

Todays Project will be about Badminton.

We will be learning how to improve and and how to play better in every game.

1.Serving

In badminton, the serve is the shot that puts the shuttlecock into play to start every rally. Unlike tennis, where serving is an aggressive weapon to win immediate points, a badminton serve is heavily regulated to prevent the server from having an unfair advantage.

2.Smash

The smash is badminton's ultimate offensive weapon. Hit overhead with maximum power, steep angle, and high shuttle speed, its goal is to force a weak lift, force an error, or win the point outright.

3.FootWork

Footwork is the backbone of badminton. Great footwork ensures you reach every shot quickly, strike from a balancedposition, and return to the center court without tiring out.
